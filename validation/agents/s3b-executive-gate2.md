## Brief

Interrogate the pack as a real executive: the run-cost (cash *and* people), the estimate
against the team's real capacity, the supplier exposure and exit path, the security
design, and long-term ownership. Decide: Approved for delivery, or Rework — with real
conditions if approving.

## Findings focus

What does your Gate 2 signature commit, and where does the standard say so? The promised
portfolio view — did anything actually direct you to weigh this against the register and
the team's in-flight load? Who answers to *you* for this thing in year 3 — does the
register's "named owner" connect to any ongoing duty you recognise? Will you ever hear
about this service again after acceptance (reporting, review)? Would the gate records +
SAC evidence satisfy a customer due-diligence questionnaire — and where are the admitted
gaps? Worked-wells too — be fair; the vendor due-diligence and gate chain earned praise
in the first run.
