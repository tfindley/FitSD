# S5c — The product manager is asked to own it

**Seat:** a product manager, formally asked to take **long-term product ownership** of the
new service: roadmap, the backlog of feature requests already arriving from consuming
teams, adoption targets, prioritisation of enhancements against the wider portfolio.
**Runs after:** S4, in parallel with S5a and S5b. **Reads:** `{SCENARIO}`,
`validation/findings-template.md`, the adoption pack, the gate records, the acceptance
record and user guide, the standard at `{STANDARD}` — read it specifically for what it
offers this seat: product, roadmap, backlog, enhancement, feedback, prioritisation.
**Writes:** `{RUN}/artifacts/S5-review-pm.md` (your considered answer, addressed to the
manager); `{RUN}/findings/S5-pm.md`.

## Brief

Give a professional answer: will you take product ownership, and on what terms? Ground it
in what you found — if you took it, what FitSD machinery would you actually hold? Which
register names you? Where does the backlog live? Who funds an enhancement, and when does
one trip the net-new line back through Gate 1? If declining or accepting conditionally,
state exactly what's missing that would change the answer.

## Findings focus

Does "product" exist in FitSD as a thing with a future, or only a service that is run,
changed and one day retired? Where do consumers' feature requests *go*, and who ranks them
— across the net-new/enhancement split? Is any role accountable for value-over-time,
distinct from the Service Owner's operational accountability? Does anything measure
adoption or satisfaction? Does the renew/replace/retire loop connect to anything a PM
recognises? Worked-wells too — the first run's PM called Gate 1 "a better business case
than most product organisations use"; check whether you agree.

## Constraints

In character in the memo; out of character in findings. British English. Do not modify
the standard or files you didn't create. Read only the files listed above.
