# S5d — The Approver decides Service Acceptance

**Seat:** the team manager as **Approver for Service Acceptance** (per the adoption pack —
and mind the builder rule: whoever built it doesn't solely approve it). There is schedule
pressure: consumers are waiting on a published go-live date. **Runs after:** S5a–c.
**Reads:** `{SCENARIO}`, `validation/findings-template.md`, the adoption pack, the
submitted acceptance record, the three reviews, the Gate 2 sign-off (its conditions come
due now), the standard at `{STANDARD}` — especially FSD-PRO §7's outcomes and FSD-FRM-03.
**Writes:** the sign-off *in* `{RUN}/artifacts/S5-FRM-03-acceptance-record.md` — decision,
conditions (each with owner and target date), the service-register entry naming the live
**Service Owner** (and deputy if the baseline requires one), header Status;
`{RUN}/findings/S5d-approver.md`.

## Brief

Make the real managerial decision, weighing the reviewers' verdicts and the executive's
at-acceptance conditions against the go-live date. If your reviewers said "accept with
conditions" — the modal real-world outcome — notice how well the process supports
recording exactly that, and do it faithfully: every surviving condition carried with an
owner, a date, and a stated place it will be tracked.

## Findings focus

Did the available outcomes fit the decision you actually faced? At the Solution Owner →
Service Owner hand-off moment, what did the standard give you — who names the live owner,
by what act does accountability transfer, and what does the outgoing Solution Owner still
owe? Who owns tracking the conditions after go-live — does anything ever re-check them?
What does *your* signature commit you to? Worked-wells too — in the first run the ratified
baseline made this decision "near-mechanical", and the operator-acceptance clause summoned
the two most valuable reviews; test both claims.

## Constraints

In character in the artifact edit; out of character in findings. British English. Do not
modify the standard or files you didn't create except the acceptance record. Read only
the files listed above.
