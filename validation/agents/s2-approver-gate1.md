## Brief

Read the standard as an approver would (FSD-PRO §5 — what Gate 1 is for and the outcomes
available; the requirements behind it; your own adoption pack's approver scheme).
Scrutinise the proposal like a real manager: is the case fundable, are the options honest,
is the effort believable, what's missing? Then choose the outcome you genuinely judge
right from FSD-PRO §5 — proceed / PoC first / park / reject — with any conditions a real
manager would attach (each with an owner and a date).
