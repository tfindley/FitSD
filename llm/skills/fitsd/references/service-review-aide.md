# Mode: service review preparation (the FSD-GV-5 walk)

**Job:** assemble the input pack for the management-system review — the "Check" of PDCA —
so the meeting reads state instead of reconstructing it. Also the right mode for the
event-driven checks: a leaver, an ownership change, a supplier EOL. Run it on a schedule
and this is the conditions sweep; run it before the review and it's the agenda.

## The per-service walk

For every live service on the register:

- **Register accuracy** (FSD-GV-4): owner still employed and in role? Deputy named where
  the continuity rule requires one? Status current, EOL column honest?
- **Owner transitions** (FSD-GV-8): any change or departure of an accountable owner since
  last review — and was continuity re-established and re-evidenced within the defined
  period (handover, successor walk-through, deputy re-named, owned register entries
  transferred)? A leaver's name still owning risks or services is a finding, not a detail.
- **Open conditions** (FSD-SD-7): every condition on every accepted record — closed on
  time / late / silently dead, with owners. Overdue ones surface as escalations to the
  approver who set them, not as agenda lines.
- **Overdue evidence**: restore tests, continuity re-checks, access reviews, supplier
  reviews (FSD-SC-4) past their baseline cadence. A date nothing reads is a wish — read
  the dates.
- **Service levels** (FSD-GV-9): statement published to the customers? Performance
  reported at the stated cadence? What did the customers actually receive since last
  review?
- **EOL candidates** (FSD-RR-7): support horizons, renewals, drift from how the service
  was accepted — anything due a renew / replace / retire conversation.

## System-level checks

- The SAC baseline: due re-ratification? Amendments queued from live friction?
- The review itself: is it happening by its date, attended by an authority the chair
  answers to? (A missed review escalates — and cannot mark its own homework.)
- The demand register: parked items past their revisit dates; the below-the-line
  enhancement queue actually being triaged by someone named.

## Output

One pack: a per-service table (the walk above, one row per service, exceptions bolded), a
conditions ledger (closed/late/dead with owners), an overdue-evidence list, and a proposed
action list — every action with a suggested owner and date, all decisions left to the
room. Where the period included real incidents or a succession, grade the system against
the standard's own promises; the record will tell you whether "no 2 a.m. page for
something nobody wrote down" held.
